# INTERN — editorial book plan

## Scope and source

User's final direction: a general representation of the GAHK internal website, not a
compilation of every stored photo or file. The wiki is entirely excluded. Fotoalbum and
Arkiv appear as overview pages only. Use the scoped local archive as immutable source
material; prepare all print assets separately under `book/`.

The site combines a personal dashboard and directory; ephemeral chats and longer notices;
events and a calendar; room allocation and room checks; work credits and a cellar account;
repairs; a fylgje family tree; statistics; and archive entry points. Administrative links
remain labels in the site map, without reproducing application records or editing screens.

## Editorial structure — 32 A5 pages

1. Front cover: INTERN / GAHK, typographic and restrained.
2. Title and dated edition note, explaining the edited snapshot.
3. Contents, organized as Oversigt / Samtaler / Huset / Ressourcer.
4–7. Dashboard facsimile and greeting; directory structure and a labeled row selection;
   the owner's profile as an example of the page type.
8–10. Den Hurtige: a short source quotation, selected actual messages, and the five channels.
11–14. Opslagstavlen: the Markdown remark, original notice text, the group-photo invitation
   as text, and examples of everyday collective life. No album photographs.
15–16. Begivenheder and Kalender, using the recorded event details and visible calendar state.
17–18. Room quotient and Værelsestjek, including original small floor-plan graphics.
19–20. AK-krydser and Ølkælder, with faithful, clearly labeled ledger selections.
21–22. Reparationer and the complete short Papirautomat discussion.
23–24. Stamtræ: the source introduction and selected actual nested family branches,
   explicitly presented as an excerpt, not a complete family tree.
25. Statistik, typeset from the original university-count table without normalizing labels.
26. Fotoalbum overview, with its title, year labels and selected distinctive album labels.
27. Arkiv overview, preserving the three visible folder labels; no stored files.
28. Site navigation map, including administrative destinations as labels only.
29. Source index with page references and readable internal paths.
30. Colophon, editorial scope, capture date, credits and edition details.
31. Farewell quotation from the repair discussion: “Hey selv tak. Hav' det smukt.”
32. Back cover.

The page map may be adjusted after pagination and visual QA, while remaining divisible by four.

## Visual language

- Forest green, warm white, pale sage and restrained brass accents, derived from the site.
- The compact GAHK/INTERN identity, fine rules, small line symbols and grouped navigation.
- EB Garamond for text and large display quotations; Manrope for labels, tables and folios.
  Both are local, open-licensed font files, with the license texts included.
- A5, exactly 148 × 210 mm; normal sequential pages. Inner margin 19 mm, outer 14 mm,
  top 15 mm, bottom 18 mm. Large decorative fields stay inside a 9 mm outer safety area.
- A consistent single-column reading measure, occasional two-column indexes, and very
  short tables. Body text approximately 11.5–12 pt; tables 9–10 pt; metadata 7.5–8 pt.
- Mirrored running headers and folios. Cover and title matter count in the PDF pagination;
  visible folios are suppressed on covers and deliberate display pages.

## Screenshots and images

Use only a few purposeful interface facsimiles. They illustrate the appearance of the site;
the surrounding text provides readable information. Do not shrink a whole long page into
an unreadable substitute for its content. The dashboard's unavailable external calendar
is omitted from its print facsimile with a clear caption. Screenshots may be cropped in a
book-specific, explicitly labeled view, preserving the untouched archive screenshots.

No photos or files from Fotoalbum or Arkiv. The five small original room-plan diagrams may
be used at modest sizes, without invented labels or enlarged pixels. No generated images.
Profile contact details and dashboard/calendar credentials are not printed. Omissions
and selections are disclosed; original spelling, punctuation and jokes are preserved.

## Decisions made during proofing

The final book remains 32 pages. The source floor-plan bitmaps contain labels too small for
a comfortable A5 reading size, so page 18 instead typesets the original floor and room numbers
as a readable overview, followed by three source check rows. No floor-plan raster is printed.
The directory excerpt uses 12 rows and the cellar account uses its 10 newest rows. The source
index uses two columns. Static font instances replace variable font loading so text embeds as
TrueType subsets; the few original emoji use embedded glyph programs. The final artifact has
three screenshot excerpts and no photographs. Every screenshot excerpt is labeled and paired
with readable typeset information.
